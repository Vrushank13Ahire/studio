IIStudio

We don't build funnels. We build rooms people want to walk through twice — then send to a friend without being asked to.

Perihelion is the point in an orbit closest to the light. We named the studio after it because that's the only brief we ever take on twice: get the thing closer to the light, then get out of the way.

Every engagement starts as a question about composition, pacing and restraint — and ends as a website, a brand system, or a piece of interactive work that didn't exist before.

VIProcess
  1. 01

    Discover

    We sit inside the problem before we touch a single pixel — audience, tone, constraints.

  2. 02

    Design

    Composition, type and colour explored as a system, not a single homepage mock.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Hand-coded, framework-free where it counts, and fast on the devices people actually use.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Launch, then a quiet week of watching, listening and sanding down the rough edges.
